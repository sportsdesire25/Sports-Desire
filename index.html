About Sports Desire

Sports Desire is India's premier tournament aggregation platform. Our mission is to bridge the gap between talented athletes and sports organizers.

Whether it's an official National Championship, a State Ranking event, or a local weekend cup, we ensure players never miss an opportunity to compete and showcase their skills.

Terms & Conditions

1. About Sports Desire

Sports Desire is the premier sports tournament aggregation and discovery platform for Northeast India (Assam, Meghalaya, Arunachal Pradesh, Nagaland, Manipur, Mizoram, Tripura, and Sikkim). Our mission is to connect athletes, parents, and coaches with verified state, national, and local tournaments.

2. 100% Free for Players & Parents

Access to tournament calendars, schedules, categories, age groups, and organizer contact details is completely free of charge for players and parents. Sports Desire does not charge players any membership or subscription fees.

3. Tournament Organiser Verified Listings (₹499 Fee)

Tournament organizers may list verified events on Sports Desire for a one-time listing verification fee of ₹499 per tournament. This fee covers digital publication, promotion across the Northeast player network, and direct WhatsApp player registration routing. Organizers are solely responsible for ensuring the accuracy of dates, age categories, rules, and venue details.

4. Match Entry Fees & Registrations

Tournament match entry fees (if applicable) are paid directly by players to tournament organizers. Sports Desire does not collect or hold player match fees. Any disputes regarding match scheduling, draws, venue logistics, or prize money must be addressed directly with the designated tournament director.

Sports Desire acts solely as a discovery and informational network. Participation in athletic competitions involves inherent physical risks. Sports Desire accepts no liability for personal injuries, equipment damage, tournament postponements, weather cancellations, or disputes arising at physical tournament grounds.
